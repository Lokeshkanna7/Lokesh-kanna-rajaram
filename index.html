04 / Hackathon spotlight

SupeRR Selector · Season 2

Rajasthan Royals / IPL 2027 auction analytics

Finding reliable performers under pressure.

Developed a transparent, context-adjusted player ranking methodology from organizer-supplied IPL and T20I ball-by-ball datasets, with a prospective squad-fit analysis for Rajasthan Royals.

193,287Valid deliveries
60,233High-pressure deliveries
20Shortlisted players
6 CSV sources→Clean & validate→Pressure index→Contextual value→Rank & squad fit

Methodology

Pre-delivery pressure scoring uses innings phase, wickets lost and recent wickets. Historical expectations use earlier match dates, with shrinkage, minimum sample thresholds and scenario testing to assess reliability.

P = min(1, B + 0.42W + 0.28R)
High pressure: P ≥ 0.55

Decision support

Separate top-10 batter and bowler lists, with conditional auction-fit recommendations emphasizing death bowling, middle-overs wicket-taking and finishing depth.
